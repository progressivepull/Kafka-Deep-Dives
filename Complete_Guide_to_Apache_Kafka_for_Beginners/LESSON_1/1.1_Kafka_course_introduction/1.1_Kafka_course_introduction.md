# Apache Kafka for Beginners — Course Introduction Summary

## Course Overview

**Course:** Apache Kafka Series — Kafka for Beginners (Third Edition)  

This introductory lesson welcomes students to the course, explains what has changed in the third edition, introduces the instructor, and describes how Conduktor will support hands-on learning.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. What's New in the Third Edition?

- The course has been freshly recorded and reorganized based on student feedback.
- It includes additional sections and lectures, plus more real-world exercises.
- The material has been updated for **Apache Kafka 3+**.
- The instructor expects much of the material to remain applicable to Kafka 4, but presents that as an expectation rather than a guarantee.
- At the time of the recording, the instructor reported **over 130,000 students** and **more than 30,000 reviews**.

### 2. About the Instructor

**Stephane Maarek** is:

- The course instructor and a co-founder of **Conduktor**.
- An online instructor specializing in Apache Kafka and AWS.
- A former Kafka Summit program committee member (2019 and 2020).
- A creator of multiple courses in the *Apache Kafka Series*.
- A technical writer and contributor to online publications.

### 3. What Is Conduktor?

**Conduktor** is a company co-founded by the instructor to make Apache Kafka easier to access and use.

Its platform includes:

- A graphical user interface (GUI) for working with Kafka.
- Tools for interacting with Kafka and its surrounding ecosystem.
- Enterprise-oriented features built around Kafka workflows.
- Support for connections to a variety of Kafka clusters and security configurations.

### 4. How Conduktor Is Used in the Course

According to the introduction, students will use Conduktor to:

1. Explore Apache Kafka through a graphical interface.
2. Practice with a free personal Kafka cluster.
3. Optionally collaborate using an organization cluster.
4. Develop skills that can improve their work with Kafka after the course.

*Note: Free cluster availability and product features are described as they were in the recording and may have changed.*

## Why This Introduction Matters

The lesson sets expectations for a **hands-on, beginner-friendly Kafka course**. It emphasizes practical exercises, refreshed explanations, and access to tools that help students experiment with Kafka rather than only study theory.

# 2.10 Theory roundup

## Overview
This lecture reviews all the core concepts covered in the Kafka theory section.

## Kafka Cluster Fundamentals
- A Kafka cluster consists of **multiple brokers** (example given: 9 brokers).
- Key concepts within the cluster:
  - **Topics**
  - **Partitions**
  - **Replication**
  - **Partition leaders** and **in-sync replicas (ISRs)**
  - The internal **`__consumer_offsets`** topic

## Producers
- Producers take data from a source system and send it into Apache Kafka.
- Key producer concepts covered:
  - **Round-robin distribution** – data is spread across all partitions in a topic.
  - **Key-based ordering** – messages with the same key always go to the same partition.
  - **Acks strategy**:
    - `acks=0`
    - `acks=1`
    - `acks=all`  
    Controls how much acknowledgment is required when writing to the cluster.

## Consumers
- Consumers operate in **consumer groups**.
- Offsets are stored in the **`__consumer_offsets`** topic.
- Consumption modes:
  - **At-least-once**
  - **At-most-once**
  - **Exactly-once**

## Cluster Management
- Traditionally managed by **ZooKeeper**:
  - Leader–follower concept
  - Broker management
  - Metadata management
- The community is transitioning away from ZooKeeper to **KRaft mode**, where the cluster is managed entirely by Kafka itself.
